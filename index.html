How to Play Gin Rummy

The Goal

Form melds and reduce unmatched "deadwood" points below your opponent's.

Melds

Set3–4 cards of the same rank
Run3+ consecutive cards of the same suit

Ending the Round

KnockEnd when deadwood ≤ 10. Score the difference.
Gin!Knock with 0 deadwood for a 25-point bonus.
UndercutIf AI deadwood ≤ yours after knock, AI scores 25 pts.

Winning

First to 100 points wins the round and earns chips.
