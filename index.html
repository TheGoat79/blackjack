How to Play Blackjack

The Goal

Beat the dealer by getting closer to 21 without going over. Exceed 21 and you bust automatically.

Card Values

2–10Face value
J, Q, K10
Ace1 or 11
SuitsDon't matter

Your Actions

HitTake another card.
StandEnd your turn.
DoubleDouble your bet, take exactly one card, then stand.

Payouts

Win2× bet
Blackjack2.5× bet
PushBet back

How to Play Gin Rummy

The Goal

Form melds (sets or runs) with your 10 cards and reduce your unmatched "deadwood" points to zero — or below your opponent's.

Melds

Set3 or 4 cards of the same rank (e.g. 7♠ 7♥ 7♦)
Run3+ consecutive cards of the same suit (e.g. 4♣ 5♣ 6♣)

Card Values

A1 pt
2–10Face value
J, Q, K10 pts

Your Turn

1. Draw from the deck or take the top discard.
2. Select a card in your hand to discard (it highlights when selected).
3. Click Discard Selected.

Ending the Round

KnockEnd the round when your deadwood is 10 or less. If yours is lower than the AI's, you score the difference.
Gin!Knock with 0 deadwood for a 25-point bonus on top of the difference.
UndercutIf the AI's deadwood is equal or lower after your knock, they score 25 pts instead.

Winning

First player to reach 100 points wins the game.
