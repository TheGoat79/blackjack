How to Play Blackjack

The Goal

Beat the dealer by getting closer to 21 without going over.

Card Values

2–10Face value
J, Q, K10
Ace1 or 11

Actions

HitTake another card.
StandEnd your turn.
DoubleDouble bet, one card, stand.

Payouts

Win2× bet
Blackjack2.5× bet
PushBet back

How to Play Gin Rummy

The Goal

Form melds and reduce unmatched "deadwood" below your opponent's.

Melds

Set3–4 cards of the same rank
Run3+ consecutive cards of the same suit

Ending the Round

KnockEnd when deadwood ≤10. Score the difference.
Gin!0 deadwood — 25-point bonus.
UndercutAI deadwood ≤ yours — AI scores 25 pts.

Winning

First to 100 points wins the round and earns chips.

How to Play I Doubt It

The Goal

Be the first player to empty your hand completely.

Each Turn

The current rank cycles A → 2 → 3 → ... → K → A. On your turn, select 1–4 cards and click Play Selected. You claim they are all the current rank — but you can lie!

I Doubt It!

CorrectThe last player was bluffing — they take the whole pile.
WrongThey were honest — you take the whole pile.

Chips

Win and earn chips equal to the largest pile during the game × 10. Lose and you pay the same amount.

How to Play Texas Hold'em

The Goal

Make the best 5-card hand using any combination of your 2 hole cards and the 5 community cards.

Betting Rounds

Pre-FlopHole cards dealt. Small & big blind posted.
Flop3 community cards revealed.
Turn4th community card revealed.
River5th community card. Final betting.
ShowdownBest hand wins the pot.

Your Actions

FoldGive up your hand and forfeit bets.
CheckPass (only when no bet to call).
CallMatch the current bet.
RaiseIncrease the bet by your raise amount.

Hand Rankings (best to worst)

Royal Flush > Straight Flush > Four of a Kind > Full House > Flush > Straight > Three of a Kind > Two Pair > One Pair > High Card

Blinds

Small blind = 10 chips, Big blind = 20 chips. Dealer position rotates each hand.
