How to Play Gin Rummy

The Goal

Form melds and reduce unmatched "deadwood" points below your opponent's.

Melds

Set3–4 cards of the same rank
Run3+ consecutive cards of the same suit

Ending the Round

KnockEnd when deadwood ≤ 10. Score the difference.
Gin!Knock with 0 deadwood for a 25-point bonus.
UndercutIf AI deadwood ≤ yours, AI scores 25 pts.

Winning

First to 100 points wins the round and earns chips.

How to Play BS

The Goal

Get rid of all your cards first. The deck is dealt evenly between all 4 players.

How It Works

Each turn, a rank is called in order (Aces, then 2s, 3s... up to Kings, then back to Aces). The current player must place 1–4 cards face-down onto the pile and claim they are that rank. They can lie!

Calling BS

Call BSChallenge the last player's claim at any time.
Caught!If they were lying, they take the whole pile.
Wrong!If they were honest, YOU take the whole pile.

Chips

Win the game and earn chips equal to the largest pile size during the game × 10. Lose and you pay that amount.

Winning

First player to empty their hand wins!
